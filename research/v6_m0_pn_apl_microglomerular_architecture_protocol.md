## Purpose
Before any V6 numerical model is run, test whether adult Drosophila mushroom-body calyx biology independently supports a materially different topology from the closed V5 shared-kappa family: direct projection-neuron (PN) recruitment of APL inside PN–KC microglomeruli, alongside KC→APL feedback.

## Frozen source-only criteria
M0 architecture support requires:
A. nonzero direct PN→APL and APL→PN contacts in adult calyx data with explicit direction;
B. the reciprocal motif in at least three distinct identifiable PN neurons/classes;
C. adult physiology independently supporting PN recruitment/local coupling of APL in the calyx;
D. official MaleCNS identity, direction, synapse coordinates and calyx/ROI fields sufficient to implement PN→APL without invented outcome-derived regions.

Direct functional APL→PN presynaptic inhibition is not authorized from anatomy alone. No synapse-count-to-current conversion, coupling parameter, V5 residual selection, MNQ/market input, reward, PnL, strategy or prop-firm result is allowed in this source-only gate.
