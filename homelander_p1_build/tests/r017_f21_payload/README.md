# R017 — F21 read-only DamageAndHit payload may not borrow another player's F17-F20 proof

Original stage F21 accepted current player B with a fresh F11 eye and hit on NPC even when the entire successful F17 damage, F18 impact, F19 continuity and F20 dry-run belong to player A. Its validations compared only target NPC handles, not the original source player; this creates a cross-player hit-direction payload falsely labeled as approved by upstream checks. Original F21 also did not track its own attempt identity or the verified F11 eye-measurement epoch across native callbacks, so an older same-player F21 could publish after a nested newer F21 or after the eye reading was replaced during a health getter.

The isolated guarded F21 source requires exact identity across current player, F17 original player/target, F18 success, F19 originating player/target and valid continuity, F20 originating player/target and dry-run PASS, plus a current F11 EYEPOINT measurement generation bound to the same player. It clears old output proof on each attempt, advances monotonic F21 generation, and rechecks all source and eye tokens after callback-heavy camera/LOS/joint/health/validity phases before publishing a read-only payload. This new F21 does NOT call any native damage, world-effect or physics setter.

Actual-source regression reuses full paired R016 F17->F18->F19->F20 source mock chain: proves old F21 accepts B using A provenance, new rejects B, same-A F11+F21 positive proof is bound to exact eye and F21 attempt generation, health callback player switch and same-player newer F11 invalidate the old payload, nested newer same-player F21 prevents outer stale overwrite.
